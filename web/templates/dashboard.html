❓ How to use Posit+EV

📐 What is +EV betting?

Every sportsbook builds a profit margin (called the vig) into their odds. But books aren't perfect — sometimes they price an outcome lower than its true probability. That gap is your edge.

+EV (positive expected value) means that if you placed this bet thousands of times, you'd profit on average. It doesn't guarantee a win tonight — it means the math is in your favor over time, the same way a casino's edge works for them.

Example: DraftKings offers +145 on the Bruins. Our model says the true probability is 46% — but +145 only implies 41%. That 5-point gap is your edge. Bet enough of these and the math compounds in your favor.

🃏 Reading a bet card

EV% — the expected profit per $100 wagered. A 5% EV bet returns $5 on average per $100 staked. Higher is better; anything above 3% is meaningful.

True Probability — the real estimated probability of this outcome, calculated by removing the vig from the sharpest books in the market (Pinnacle, NoVig, exchanges). This is our best estimate of what will actually happen.

Implied Probability — what the sportsbook's odds imply the probability is. When this is lower than the True Probability, you have an edge.

Odds — American odds offered by the book. Positive odds (+150) mean you win $150 per $100 staked. Negative odds (-120) mean you stake $120 to win $100.

¼ Kelly % — the recommended percentage of your bankroll to stake on this bet. See the Kelly section below for more detail.

📊 Market types

Moneyline Spread Total Team Total NRFI / YRFI Player Props

Moneyline — bet on who wins outright. Spread — bet on the margin of victory (e.g. Team A -3.5 must win by 4+). Total (O/U) — combined score over or under a number. Team Total — one team's score over or under. NRFI — No Run First Inning; YRFI — Yes Run First Inning.

Player Props — bets on individual player statistics: points, rebounds, assists, goals, shots, strikeouts, home runs, etc. These often have more pricing errors than game markets because books spend less time sharpening them.

📈 CLV — Closing Line Value

The closing line is the final odds a book posts before a game starts. Sharp bettors consistently beat the closing line — meaning they get better odds than the final market price. CLV is the gold standard for evaluating whether your bets are good, independent of wins and losses.

The CLV pill on each card shows how much the line has moved since opening. A green pill means the line moved in your favor (the market confirmed your edge). A red pill means it moved against you.

Example: You bet +145 at open. The line closes at +120. The book sharpened the line toward your bet — that's +CLV confirmed, a strong signal your model is beating the market.

🔥 Sharp money signals

Bet % — the percentage of all bets placed on this side (from Action Network public data). When a small % of bettors are on a side but it's getting most of the money, that's a sharp signal.

Money % — the percentage of total dollars wagered on this side. High money % with low bet % means large, sophisticated bettors are on this side — a classic sharp money indicator.

Sharp Score — our composite signal (0–100) combining money vs. bet split, line movement direction, and market timing. Scores above 70 suggest professional money is active.

🔥 Sharp move badge — appears on prop cards when the implied probability has steamed 3+ percentage points since opening, indicating coordinated sharp action on this line.

⚡ Sharp Action badge — appears on game cards when professional bettors (sharps) appear to be on the same side as our model. It combines two signals: (1) a low % of the public is betting this side — sharps tend to bet against the crowd — and (2) the line has moved in our favor since opening, meaning the market is pricing in sharp money. When both signals align, it's a stronger indication the edge is real.

🏆 Sharp Grade (S → D)

Every bet card carries a Sharp Grade — a single letter that blends all seven conviction signals into one rating so you know at a glance where to focus.

S — All signals firing: strong EV, CLV confirmed, sharp money aligned, and market consensus. Highest conviction picks on the board.

A — Most signals positive with at least one strong conviction indicator. Solid edge worth acting on.

B — Decent edge with partial signal confirmation. Good value, less certainty.

C / D — Weaker signal stack. The math still shows edge, but fewer signals confirm it. Size down.

Quality filter: S-grade picks where the line has moved against the position are automatically hidden. If the model says S but the market is pricing it the other way, that contradiction disqualifies the pick from showing.

🤖 +EV Insight (AI Analysis)

Click Analyze on any bet card to generate a full AI breakdown. It takes 10–20 seconds the first time; results are cached for 6 hours.

Confidence Score (0–100) — aggregates edge strength, sharp money confirmation, line movement, and real-world context into a single conviction rating. 75+ is high conviction; below 50 means proceed cautiously.

Kelly sizing — AI-calibrated stake recommendation for this specific bet, not a flat formula.

The analysis includes team form, injury signals, game-log context for props, and a plain-English explanation of why the edge exists.

⚾ HR Model

The ⚾ HR Model tab gives you dedicated home run prop picks scored by a purpose-built MLB probability engine — separate from the main props feed. Home run props only appear here, not in the Props tab.

Each pick is scored using four independent factors:

Batter HR rate — season HR per plate appearance, blended with recent 15-game form (70/30 split) to balance sample size with current hot/cold streaks.

Pitcher HR/9 — how many home runs the opposing pitcher allows per 9 innings vs. the league average (1.30). A pitcher at 1.70 HR/9 is 31% more homer-prone than average — that directly lifts the model probability.

Park factor — each stadium has a multi-year HR park factor. Coors Field (+18%) and Great American Ball Park (+13%) are extreme hitter-friendly; AT&T Park (-16%) suppresses homers significantly.

Wind — live wind speed and direction from game-day weather. Strong winds blowing out add up to +6% to the model probability; indoor parks are unaffected.

Picks are ranked by composite edge score and each card shows an AI-written rationale explaining the key factors driving the value.

Example: Aaron Judge vs. a 1.65 HR/9 pitcher at +185 (+18% edge). Model: 44% · Book: 35.1% · +8.9pp edge. Scores are refreshed every 30 minutes, immediately after each cache update.

📐 Kelly Criterion & bankroll sizing

The Kelly Criterion is a formula that calculates the mathematically optimal bet size based on your edge and the odds. Betting full Kelly is theoretically perfect but extremely volatile in practice.

We use ¼ Kelly — the industry standard among professional bettors. It captures most of the long-run growth while dramatically reducing the chance of a bad run wiping out your bankroll.

Set your bankroll in the sidebar (desktop) or filters panel (mobile) and every card will show you a dollar amount to stake.

Example: $1,000 bankroll. A bet shows ¼ Kelly: 2.5% → stake $25 on this bet.

⚡ Prediction markets & betting exchanges

Kalshi Polymarket NoVig ProphetX BetOpenly

Prediction markets (Kalshi, Polymarket) are CFTC-regulated and available in all 50 states. You're trading contracts rather than betting with a sportsbook — no vig, just market pricing.

Betting exchanges (NoVig, ProphetX, BetOpenly) match bettors directly against each other with ~1–2% commission instead of the typical 5–10% sportsbook vig. They never limit winning bettors — a major advantage if you're consistently profitable.

These platforms also serve as our sharp-market reference for calculating true probability on every pick — including traditional sportsbook bets.

🎰 Parlay builder

Click Parlay in the toolbar to enter parlay mode. Select 2–5 bet cards and the builder automatically calculates the combined odds, combined EV%, and the Kelly-sized stake for the parlay as a whole.

Note: parlays are generally lower EV than individual bets because the vig compounds across legs. Only use this for bets where you have strong conviction on each leg.

📊 Bet Tracker

Click Tracker in the toolbar to log bets and track your results over time. Add any bet — on or off the dashboard — with the stake, odds, and outcome. The tracker calculates your running P&L, ROI, and win rate.

Your tracked bets also show a CLV result once the game starts, so you can see whether you consistently beat the closing line (the most reliable long-run signal of a profitable model).

🔍 Filters & sorting

Use the league chips (NHL, NBA, MLB, etc.) to filter by sport. Use the market chips (Moneyline, Spread, Props, etc.) to filter by bet type. Use the book filter to show only bets available at specific sportsbooks you have accounts with.

The 🎯 Props pill shows all player props except home runs. The ⚾ HR Model pill switches to the dedicated home run model view — click it again to return to the main feed.

Sort by EV% High→Low to see your best mathematical edges first. Sort by Game Time to see bets starting soonest. The EV% slider in the sidebar lets you raise the minimum threshold to see only higher-confidence edges.

📐 Kelly Criterion

The Kelly Criterion is a mathematical formula that calculates the optimal percentage of your bankroll to bet, based on your true edge and the odds offered. It maximizes long-term growth without risking ruin.

Full Kelly is theoretically optimal but dangerously volatile — a string of bad beats can cause devastating drawdowns, even with a real edge.

Fractional Kelly (½, ¼, ⅛) is used by professional bettors and hedge funds to reduce variance while still growing the bankroll efficiently. ¼ Kelly is the industry standard — it captures most of the growth with dramatically less risk.

Full Kelly (1×) Highest growth, highest variance
½ Kelly Aggressive, lower drawdowns
¼ Kelly Professional standardRECOMMENDED
⅛ Kelly Conservative, minimal variance

Use a smaller fraction when you're less certain about the model's edge, or when you're new to EV betting. You can always adjust as you gain experience.
